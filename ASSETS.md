# Asset provenance

- `public/images/wedding-arch.webp`: project-specific original image generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Prompt: “Premium fine-art wedding invitation still life, white roses and orchids with olive branches around a warm ivory plaster arch, flowing beige silk, diffused daylight, empty center, no people, text or watermark, portrait.” The generated PNG was encoded as WebP for delivery.
- `public/music/this-too-shall-pass.mp3`: “This Too Shall Pass” by Scott Buckley, released under CC BY 4.0. Official source: https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/this-too-shall-pass/ . License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Full recording retained, re-encoded at 160 kbps. Attribution is visible in the site footer and included in `public/music/CREDITS.txt`.
- `public/music/our-moment.mp3` (optional former track): original instrumental synthesis made for this project. Reproducible source in `scripts/create_music.py`; 58.3 seconds, stereo MP3.
- `public/fonts/`: Google Fonts Cormorant Garamond regular/italic and Manrope regular/semibold, converted losslessly from TTF to WOFF containers. License texts included alongside fonts.
- Decorative line-art and envelope are project-native SVG/CSS. Icons come from lucide-react (ISC license).
